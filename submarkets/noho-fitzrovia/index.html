How the scores are calculated

Connectivity

Measures public transport accessibility within the submarket:

Amenity

Captures depth of everyday amenities:

  • Total 4–5★ hotel rooms
  • Number of gyms
  • Total Food & Beverage outlets
  • Total garden-square acreage

Culture

  • Number of theatres
  • Number of nightclubs
